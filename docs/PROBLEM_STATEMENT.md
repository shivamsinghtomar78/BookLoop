# BookLoop

> **Give your books a second life.**
> A digital platform for buying, selling, exchanging and donating old school books.

**Last updated:** 13 Sep 2026

---

## 1. The Problem

When students finish a class, most of their books are still perfectly usable — yet they end up sitting on a shelf, gathering dust, or being thrown away. At the same time, students entering that class (or their parents) spend money buying the **exact same books** brand new.

The result:

- **Money wasted** — families repeatedly pay full price for books that already exist in the community.
- **Books wasted** — usable books go unused and eventually become waste.
- **No easy connection** — there is no simple, trusted way for the student who *has* a book to reach the student who *needs* it.

---

## 2. The Solution

**BookLoop** connects students within a school so books flow from those who are done with them to those who need them — through four modes on one platform:

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🛒 **Buy** | Search and buy used books at low prices |
| 💰 **Sell** | List a finished book in minutes and earn back some of its cost |
| 🔄 **Exchange** | Swap a book you have for a book you need (e.g., Class 8 Science ⇄ Class 8 Maths) |
| 🎁 **Donate** | Give usable books free to students who need them |

Every listing gets a unique **BookLoop ID** (e.g., `BL-0042`) and a **QR code**, making each book trackable and shareable.

---

## 3. How It Works

### Selling a book
1. Student uploads the book's **title, category, class, subject, edition/year, condition and price**, with **1–4 photos** (at least one required).
2. Condition is chosen from a **fixed 4-tier standard** (see §5) — no vague descriptions, no disputes.
3. The listing goes live with its **BookLoop ID + QR code**.

### Buying a book
1. Buyer searches and filters by **category, class, subject, condition and price**.
2. Found the right book? Contact the seller through **in-app chat** — no phone numbers or emails are ever exposed.
3. Handover happens at a **designated school pickup point** (library / school office), not private meetups.
4. Seller marks the book **Sold**, buyer confirms, and both sides leave a quick 👍/👎 rating.

### Exchanging a book
- A student posts an offer: **HAS:** Class 8 Science ⇄ **WANTS:** Class 8 Maths.
- Others browse offers and chat to arrange the swap.
- *Post-pilot:* a **smart matching engine** automatically surfaces "someone wants what you have" pairs — and eventually multi-way exchange chains (A→B→C→A).

### Donating a book
- Donations are listed **free**, shown in a dedicated "Free — donations available" shelf.
- A student in need claims the book via chat; claimed books drop out of search automatically.

## 4. Book Categories

BookLoop is not only for textbooks — it covers **every type of book a student uses**, each with filters that fit that category:

| Category | Examples | Filters |
|---|---|---|
| 📘 **Textbooks** | NCERT / board books, class-wise | Class (1–12), Subject |
| 📗 **Reference & Guide books** | Extra practice, class-wise guides, question banks | Class, Subject |
| 🏆 **Competitive exam books** | JEE, NEET, Olympiad, other entrance prep | Exam, Subject |
| 📖 **Novels & other books** | Fiction, non-fiction, general reading | Genre, Author |

---

## 5. Trust & Safety (built into the design)

| Risk | BookLoop's answer |
|---|---|
| Students sharing personal contact details | **In-app chat only** — phone numbers and emails are never shown |
| Unsafe private meetups | **Designated school pickup point** (library / office) for all handovers |
| "You said good condition, this is torn" disputes | **Fixed condition tiers**: Like New · Good · Fair · Worn — with mandatory photos |
| Unreliable sellers | **👍/👎 rating** after every completed transaction builds a visible reputation |
| Fake / duplicate accounts | **One account per student ID** at full launch (see §6) |

## 6. Accounts & Verification — Phased Approach

### Phase 1 — Initial submission (prototype)
No identity verification yet — signup is fast and simple:

| Signup collects | Login uses |
|---|---|
| Full name | **Email + Password** |
| School name | |
| Age | |
| Class | |
| Email (confirmation link sent) | |
| Password (min 8 characters) | |

- An email **confirmation link** is sent at signup; unconfirmed accounts can browse but cannot list or chat.

### Phase 2 — Full launch (verified students)
Schools already hold complete student data, so verification plugs in cleanly:

1. Student enters their **11-digit Student ID + name**.
2. BookLoop matches both against the **roster provided by the school** (ID must match name — not just exist).
3. Match found → account is flagged **"Verified student"** — a badge shown on the profile and all listings.
4. **One account per student ID** — duplicates and fakes are blocked at the source.

> 🔒 **Privacy by design:** no ID-card photo uploads, ever. Only the number is matched against the school's roster — no sensitive images are stored on BookLoop's servers.

## 7. Standout Features

- **Wishlist + Notify-me** — "Tell me when a Class 9 Maths NCERT is listed." Solves the timing gap between when sellers list and when buyers look.
- **Bundle listings** — sell a full class's book set in one listing (what parents actually want at year-end).
- **Seasonal push** — notifications aligned with the school calendar: term-end = "list your books," admission season = "books available for your new class."
- **Sustainability dashboard (for schools)** — books reused, money saved, waste reduced — live impact numbers per school.
- **Gamification** — badges and a leaderboard for top donors and most books reused; makes giving visible and cool.
- **QR book history** *(future)* — scan a book's BookLoop QR to see its journey: how many students it has served.

---

## 8. Business Model

**Students never pay to use BookLoop.** A platform fee on ₹50–200 used-book transactions would punish exactly the behaviour we want to encourage — so the model monetizes institutions, not students:

| Revenue stream | When | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| 🏫 **School subscriptions** | Primary, from early on | Sustainability dashboard, CSR/eco-club reporting, verified-student integration, library integration |
| ⭐ **Featured listings** | Only after multi-school scale | Optional paid visibility boost — pointless in a small pool, valuable in a big one |

---

## 9. Launch Strategy

1. **Pilot at one school.** A closed, single-school community solves the trust problem for free and keeps handovers simple.
2. **Seed before opening.** Run a **book collection drive** with the school so the platform launches with 100+ listings already live — a marketplace must never feel empty on day one (cold-start fix).
3. **Measure.** Track listings, completed transactions, money saved and books reused during the pilot.
4. **Expand school by school**, using each pilot's sustainability numbers as the sales pitch to the next school.

---

## 10. Impact

BookLoop sits at the intersection of **technology, business and sustainability**:

- 💸 **Students save money** — used books at a fraction of retail price, or free.
- 📚 **Books get a second life** — every reuse is a purchase avoided.
- 🌱 **Less waste** — fewer books printed, fewer books thrown away.
- 🏫 **Schools gain** — a measurable, reportable sustainability initiative for their community.

**One book, many students. That's the loop.**
